How to Play

  1. Place your pieces. Each player gets only 3 pieces.
  2. Move. After placing all 3, move one of your pieces to an empty square on your turn.
  3. Create a line. Get your 3 pieces in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  4. Win. First player to make 3 in a row wins. Endless back-and-forth ends in a draw.
